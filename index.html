About me

I'm Wooseok Lee, a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at Seoul National University, supervised by Prof. Kyoung Mu Lee. My research focuses on low-level image restoration, with a particular emphasis on practical real-world image denoising.

Especially, I study self-supervised denoising methods that recover clean images from noisy observations without ground-truth (GT) clean images. I aim to develop reliable approaches that can be trained directly on real-world noisy data, where collecting paired clean targets is often impractical.
